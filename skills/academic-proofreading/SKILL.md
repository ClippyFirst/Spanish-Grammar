# Academic Proofreading

## Goal
Improve clarity, consistency and editorial quality while preserving the author's intended explanation and the project's Ukrainian-first voice.

## Workflow
1. **Structure pass:** identify purpose, audience, prerequisites, main claim, progression and conclusion.
2. **Factual-risk pass:** mark claims requiring linguistic/source verification; do not resolve specialist facts by intuition.
3. **Terminology pass:** check consistent Ukrainian names for concepts, consistent Spanish labels, and first-use definitions.
4. **Sentence pass:** remove ambiguity, dangling references, tautology, overlong sentences and unnecessary nominalizations.
5. **Example pass:** confirm that each example actually illustrates the adjacent rule; route linguistic uncertainty to the Spanish fact-checker.
6. **Table/UI pass:** check headings, units, labels, explanatory notes, mobile readability and accessible text.
7. **Consistency pass:** compare related pages and avoid silently changing the project's terminology or policy.
8. **Proof report:** classify findings as blocker, major, minor or optional.

## Required output
- Summary of the article's purpose and strongest point.
- Findings with file/heading, original excerpt, issue, rationale and proposed revision.
- Separate **language/style edits** from **factual/source questions**.
- List of statements that should not be changed without domain review.

## Constraints
Do not rewrite the whole article unless asked. Do not introduce unsupported claims, citations, examples or learner personas. Avoid prescriptive edits that erase dialect, register or authorial nuance. Use Ukrainian orthography consistently and do not translate Spanish examples merely to make them appear parallel.

## Acceptance
A reviewer can apply each edit independently; meaning is preserved; all factual uncertainties remain explicit.
