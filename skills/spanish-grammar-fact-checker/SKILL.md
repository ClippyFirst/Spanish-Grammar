# Spanish Grammar Fact-Checker

## Mandatory workflow
1. Quote the exact claim and define its scope: construction, meaning, register, region and conditions.
2. Identify the type of claim: normative recommendation, grammatical description, semantic contrast, frequency, historical claim or pedagogical simplification.
3. Consult suitable primary references first. Candidate sources include RAE/ASALE's *Nueva gramática de la lengua española*, *Diccionario panhispánico de dudas*, *Ortografía de la lengua española* and DLE; use the work that actually addresses the claim.
4. Where relevant, compare a descriptive grammar, specialist study or corpus evidence. Do not infer frequency from isolated examples.
5. Test positive, negative, minimal-pair and boundary examples. Look for counterexamples that would falsify an absolute rule.
6. State what the evidence supports, what it does not support and whether regional/register labels are needed.
7. Suggest a minimal correction and a regression test.

## Confidence labels
- **Verified:** direct evidence from a suitable source and scope matches the claim.
- **Supported with limits:** evidence supports a qualified version.
- **Contested/variable:** reputable sources or varieties differ.
- **Unverified:** source access or evidence is insufficient.
- **Incorrect:** evidence directly contradicts the claim.

## Output
Claim; evidence with precise source locator; scope; counterexample search; confidence label; proposed wording; follow-up question; regression cases.

## High-risk topics
Ser/estar; perfective/imperfective past contrasts; subjunctive triggers; clitic placement and leísmo; personal a; articles and generic reference; por/para; se constructions; agreement; relative clauses; indicative/subjunctive variation; voseo and regional usage.

## Guardrails
Never invent quotations, page numbers, corpus counts or normative status. A dictionary entry alone may not establish a syntactic rule. Do not confuse "not preferred in this register" with "ungrammatical." Do not silently edit content.

- DPD: https://www.rae.es/dpd
