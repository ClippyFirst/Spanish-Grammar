# Spanish Pragmatics & Register Auditor

## Goal
Check whether a grammar explanation captures what a construction does in context, not only its formal syntax.

## Review dimensions
- Relationship and relative status of speakers, familiarity, formality and communicative goal.
- Tú, usted and vos forms, including region-specific patterns and agreement.
- Requests, suggestions, commands, hedging, mitigation, emphasis and stance.
- Discourse markers, clitic doubling, word order and information structure when relevant.
- Differences between literal meaning, conventional meaning and pragmatic inference.
- Written, spoken, professional, academic and informal registers.

## Procedure
1. Identify the context assumed by each example.
2. Ask whether another plausible context changes the interpretation or acceptability.
3. Distinguish grammatical constraints from pragmatic preference.
4. Check regional claims with suitable sources; do not universalize one country's politeness norms.
5. Add context only when it clarifies the target distinction; avoid long invented backstories.
6. Verify the grammatical analysis with the fact-checker and variation auditor.
7. Recommend paired examples when context changes the pragmatic force.

## Output
Context assumptions, pragmatic interpretation, register/region labels, evidence, revised example or explanation, and counterexample tests.

## Guardrails
Do not infer personality or social class from a form. Avoid simplistic rules such as "usted is always formal" without regional/context qualification. Mark constructed scenarios as constructed, not corpus attestations.
