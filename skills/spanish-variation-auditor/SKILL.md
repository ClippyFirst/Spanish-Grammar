# Spanish Variation & Usage Auditor

## Scope dimensions
For every variable claim, identify relevant geography (Spain, Mexico, Rioplatense region, Caribbean, Andean region, etc.), register, genre, formality, speaker relationship and whether the variation is grammatical, lexical, pragmatic or orthographic.

## Procedure
1. Highlight universal words such as "always", "never", "all Spanish speakers" and "incorrect".
2. Determine whether the page describes the pan-Hispanic norm, one national norm, a common tendency or colloquial usage.
3. Verify the claim with RAE/ASALE resources and appropriate regional/descriptive evidence.
4. Separate normative status from frequency, prestige, formality and local preference.
5. For voseo, address relevant pronoun and verb paradigms and distinguish geographic patterns; do not treat voseo as one uniform system.
6. For leísmo/laísmo/loísmo, clitic use, past-tense preferences and subjunctive variation, define the specific construction and region.
7. Propose a label and note that preserves the main explanation while making the scope explicit.

## Output
Claim, current implied scope, evidence, variation dimensions, recommended label, revised wording and examples to test.

## Guardrails
Do not reduce Latin America to one variety. Do not mark a valid form as an error because it differs from a textbook default. Do not call every colloquial form non-standard; cite evidence. Avoid unsupported claims about social class or speaker identity.
