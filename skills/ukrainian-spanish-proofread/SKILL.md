# Ukrainian–Spanish Proofread

## Purpose
Run two separate language reviews. Ukrainian is the explanation language; Spanish is the object language. Do not let fluency in one substitute for expertise in the other.

## Pass A — Ukrainian
- Check spelling, punctuation, agreement, government, word choice and natural word order.
- Prefer precise, idiomatic Ukrainian terminology and explain specialist terms on first use.
- Remove calques, redundant transitions, false friends and ambiguous pronouns.
- Keep Ukrainian explanations concise without oversimplifying linguistic distinctions.

## Pass B — Spanish
- Check accentuation, agreement, conjugation, clitic placement, prepositions, syntax and punctuation.
- Distinguish grammaticality from idiomaticity and from frequency.
- Identify intended country/region, register and communicative context when these affect acceptability.
- Do not mark a valid regional form as an error merely because another variety is more familiar.
- Route uncertain normative questions to the Spanish Grammar Fact-Checker.

## Output
Return findings in two separate sections, each with severity, exact excerpt, explanation and minimal correction. Label Spanish items as **confirmed error**, **context-dependent**, or **requires verification**.

## Guardrails
Do not claim to provide a formal normative certification. Do not use Russian-influenced Ukrainian terminology where a standard Ukrainian equivalent is available. Do not rewrite every sentence for personal preference; focus on correctness, clarity and naturalness.
